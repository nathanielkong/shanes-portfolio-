Flowers that speaks from the heart

Oopsie Daisy began with a simple idea: flowers have a way of making ordinary moments feel special. Whether it’s a celebration, a thank-you, an apology, or simply “just because,” we believe every bouquet carries a feeling worth remembering.

The brand was applied across signage, bouquet wrapping, cards, packaging, social media, and a website mockup to show Oopsie Daisy as a complete floral studio. Each touchpoint uses the same soft visual style to create a consistent and thoughtful customer experience.

Oopsie Daisy is positioned as a floral studio, not a typical florist. It creates design-led arrangements for people who value beauty, emotion, and individuality. The brand feels soft, artistic, romantic, and memorable.
