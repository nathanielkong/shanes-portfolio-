Projects

Maruki Ramen is a rebranding project created to refresh the restaurant’s visual identity with a warmer, more modern, and memorable look. The brand focuses on the comfort of ramen culture, using a bowl, noodles, and chopsticks as the main logo elements to clearly represent Japanese food and dining.
The new identity uses a warm reddish-brown colour with cream tones to create a cosy, welcoming, and appetising feeling. The logo is designed to be simple and flexible, allowing it to work across menus, packaging, uniforms, vehicles, billboards, signage, and digital platforms.

The mockups show how the Maruki Ramen rebrand can be applied across different brand materials and customer touchpoints. The new visual identity is used on menus, packaging, uniforms, signage, chopsticks, delivery vehicles, billboards, and digital platforms. This helps the brand feel consistent, professional, and recognisable in both physical and online spaces.
