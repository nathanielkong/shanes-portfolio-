Sarawak Youth Talent is a website and booklet project created to celebrate young creative talent in Sarawak. The design uses soft natural colours, floral details, and local-inspired visuals to make the campaign feel warm, welcoming, and connected to Sarawak’s identity.

shanesoon235@gmail.com

The website introduces Sarawak Youth Talent through a warm digital experience, using natural colours, floral details, and local-inspired visuals to encourage youth participation.

The website features a responsive user interface designed for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. Inspired by Sarawak's rich biodiversity, the visual identity incorporates organic illustrations, earthy green tones, and nature-inspired elements to create a welcoming and authentic digital experience.
