Bloody Health is a conceptual vitamin supplement brand designed specifically for vampires. The project explores how traditional health and wellness branding can be reimagined within a dark fantasy context, combining gothic aesthetics with modern packaging design.


The visual identity features a rich burgundy and white colour palette inspired by blood, elegance, and luxury. Ornamental typography and vintage-inspired graphics were incorporated to create a premium and sophisticated brand experience while maintaining a playful connection to vampire culture.


The project includes packaging design, product labels, and promotional materials that work together to establish a cohesive brand identity. Through this project, I explored storytelling through branding and created a unique product concept that balances humour, fantasy, and professional design principles.
