“Kiss or Death” is a campaign against animal testing in cosmetics. The red kiss marks symbolize beauty, while the campaign message reveals the hidden cruelty behind some beauty products. It encourages people to make more compassionate choices by supporting cruelty-free brands.

shanesoon235@gmail.com

The red lipstick marks represent cosmetics and beauty, while the rabbit imagery symbolises animals commonly used in testing. The torn paper style gives the campaign a raw and emotional feeling, showing that animal testing is not glamorous or harmless. The campaign message reminds people that beauty should not come at the cost of animal suffering.

The mockups show how the campaign can be used across digital, print, and public advertising. From phone screens and websites to tote bags, posters, and billboards, the visuals help spread the message in everyday spaces and encourage people to choose cruelty-free products.
