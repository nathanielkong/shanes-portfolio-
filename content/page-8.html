Beauty In The Pot is a hotpot restaurant brand known for its nourishing soups and elegant dining experience. This project focused on creating a cohesive promotional campaign that reflects the brand's warm, welcoming, and feminine identity.


The campaign includes a series of marketing materials such as vouchers, promotional calendars, and social media assets. A soft pink colour palette, playful illustrations, and modern typography were used to reinforce the brand's recognisable visual identity while appealing to its target audience.


Through this project, I explored brand consistency across multiple touchpoints and developed a promotional system that enhances customer engagement while maintaining a strong and memorable brand presence.
