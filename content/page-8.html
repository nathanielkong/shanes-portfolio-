Soonami is a personal branding project built around the idea of creativity moving like a wave — constantly evolving, flowing, and creating new possibilities. The identity reflects a bold and energetic approach to design, with a focus on fresh ideas and expressive visual communication.


The visual system uses soft, rounded typography, wave-inspired forms, and a playful colour palette to create a brand that feels approachable, distinctive, and memorable. The tagline “A wave of ideas.” captures Soonami’s focus on creativity, experimentation, and visual storytelling.
