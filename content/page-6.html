Unbound is a magazine cover design inspired by architecture, culture, and public knowledge. The design uses a strong black, white, and red colour palette to create a bold editorial look. The building image gives the cover a formal and institutional feeling, while the red typography and graphic shapes add energy and visual impact.

‍

The layout focuses on contrast, hierarchy, and modern editorial composition. The large title immediately attracts attention, while the vertical text and layered red shapes guide the viewer’s eye through the cover. Overall, the design presents the magazine as confident, contemporary, and visually memorable.
